How to play

The city fell in one night. Fight from T. Nagar to the ruins of Mamallapuram, one wave per district. Every district has less cover than the last, and the infected get stranger the further you go.

    Survival notes

    • The pistol hits hard and saves ammo. The rifle shreds groups but drains magazines.
    • Headshots do 2.5× damage with guns.
    • From wave 4, standing still too long brings runners in from the sides. Keep moving.
    • Molotovs set a 3 m area on fire. Urundai bombs blow up on impact, so never throw one at your feet.
    • When the Raatchasan charges into a wall it is stunned. That is your window.
